Buying & Escrow

How does escrow protect me?

When you buy, your money is held safely by Marketa — the seller never receives it up front. Funds are only released to the seller after you confirm you received exactly what you ordered.

What happens after I pay?

Your money is held in escrow. The seller has 5 days to ship the item. Once shipped, you have 7 days to confirm delivery. If the seller doesn't ship in time, you're automatically refunded.

What if the item doesn't match the description?

From "My Orders", tap Request Return and pick a reason. The seller will be notified. If you can't agree, tap Escalate to Admin and our team will rule on the dispute.

How long does a refund take?

Once a refund is approved (either by the seller or by an admin ruling), funds are returned from escrow immediately. Bank settlement typically takes 1–3 business days.

What if the seller never ships?

You're fully protected. If the seller doesn't mark the order as shipped within 5 days of your payment, the order auto-cancels and your money is returned to you.

Can I extend a deadline?

Yes — both buyers and sellers get one extension per order. Sellers can add 2 days to the ship deadline; buyers can add 3 days to the confirm deadline. The option appears in the order card when 24 hours or less remain.

Selling & Listings

How do I post a listing?

Tap Sell in the bottom bar, choose a category, add up to 3 photos, set your price, quantity and location, then publish. Your ad appears instantly on the homepage.

Can I edit a listing after posting?

Yes — open My Listings and tap Edit. Note: if the listing has an active order, it can't be edited until the order is finished.

What happens when I sell all my stock?

When a listing's quantity reaches zero, it's automatically marked as Sold and removed from the public marketplace. You can always Relist it from My Listings if you get more stock.

Why was my listing removed?

A listing is removed when another user reports it and our moderation team confirms it broke our rules — for example, a prohibited item, a scam, or misleading photos. You'll see a Removed badge on the listing in My Listings.

Can I appeal a removal?

Yes. Contact us via the email or WhatsApp below with the listing name and reason. If the removal was a mistake, the listing will be restored.

Reports & Moderation

How do I report a listing or a user?

Open any listing and tap Report this listing at the bottom of the seller section. Pick a reason, add details if you'd like, and submit. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team.

What happens after I report something?

A moderator reviews the report. Depending on the outcome, the listing may be removed, the report may be dismissed (if no rules were broken), or further action may be taken against the account.

What happens if I submit a false report?

We review reports carefully. Accounts that repeatedly submit false or malicious reports may be restricted from using the report feature, or in serious cases, suspended.

What are the rules of Marketa?

No prohibited or illegal items. No scams, fakes, or misleading listings. No harassment or impersonation. Sellers must ship what they describe. Buyers must confirm receipt honestly. Breaking these rules can lead to listings being removed and accounts being restricted.

Account & Bans

What can get my account restricted or banned?

Serious or repeated rule-breaking. Examples: running scams, selling illegal items, repeatedly filing false reports, or refusing to complete orders. Depending on the severity, we may issue a warning, restrict certain features, or permanently ban the account.

What happens to my escrow funds if I'm banned?

Funds already held in escrow for legitimate in-progress orders are resolved fairly — buyers are refunded, and sellers are paid for orders that were delivered and confirmed. Any funds tied to fraudulent activity may be frozen pending review.

Can I appeal a ban?

Yes. Contact us via email or WhatsApp with your account email and a brief explanation. Our team will review the decision. Not all appeals are successful, but every appeal is read.

How do I delete my account?

Open Mine → Settings → Delete Account. Note that you must have no active orders or disputes. Deletion is permanent and removes your listings, orders, chats and reviews.

Privacy & Safety

Is my personal information safe?

Your phone number is only shown to someone buying from you — and only when they tap "Show phone number". Your email is never visible to other users. We never share your data with third parties.

How do I stay safe when meeting a seller?

Meet in a safe, public place during daytime. Inspect the item before paying. Keep the conversation and payment inside Marketa — never send money outside the platform. If something feels off, cancel the deal.

What does the "Verified" badge mean?

The ✓ Verified badge is awarded by the Marketa team to sellers we trust. These are sellers who've completed real sales and built a good track record on the platform. It's our way of helping buyers spot reliable people to deal with. If you're a seller who's been actively selling, you may be verified automatically as we grow.
